ABOUT THE PLAY

Where the pages come alive.

Across every age... humanity has never walked alone. History remembers. Science explores. Art inspires. Philosophy questions. Literature creates.

Today, these timeless companions step beyond the pages of a book and into the story of one child.

What begins as an ordinary visit to a library gradually becomes something stranger. The boundaries between the world of the child, the stories she encounters, and the library itself begin to blur.

Told through a dream-like loop and scenes that unfold within the larger story, The Library explores the strange ways in which stories, ideas and memory continue to live through those who encounter them.
